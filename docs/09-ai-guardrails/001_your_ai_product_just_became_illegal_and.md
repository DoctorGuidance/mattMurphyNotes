## 💡 2. Root Cause & Architectural Solution (Matt Murphy Analysis)
New transparency obligations are enforcable right now, and they all revolve around one term you've never heard of, SGI, synthetically generated information. So, if your product uses an AI model to write any copy, generate images, create avatars, or produce any content that your users are consuming, you are now distributing SGI. And if you do not disclose that to your users, you are now legally liable for deceptive practices in every jurisdiction that has adopted these new rules.

---

## ⚡ 3. Hardening Action Checklist
- [ ] explicit labeling on every AI generated output your product produces. Not a footnote, not a buried disclaimer, but a very visible, non removable disclosure that tells the user this content was generated by AI.
- [ ] hard consent before any user input touches an AI model. If your app takes a user's photo and runs it through a model or takes their text and rewrites it in AI, the user has to opt in explicitly, not a pre-checked box, not a buried clause in your terms of service, an affirmative action by the user before their data enters the model.
- [ ] a generation log that proves what your system produced and when. If a government audit asks what your AI generated last Tuesday, you have to have an answer.
